Step by Step Guide

How to join the test.

Follow these steps carefully. It takes less than 2 minutes and you only need to do this once per app.

01
Open Google Chrome and sign in with your Gmail

On your Android phone, open Google Chrome. Make sure you are signed in with the Gmail account you registered as a tester. Testing access is tied to your Google account — use the exact Gmail you gave to the developer.

02
Copy the testing link of the app you want

Scroll down to the apps section below. Find the app you want to test and tap the "Copy Link" button. The link will be copied to your clipboard automatically.

📋 One tap to copy — no typing needed
03
Paste the link in Google Chrome's address bar

Tap the address bar at the top of Chrome, long-press and tap "Paste", then press Enter. This takes you directly to the Google Play testing page.

📱 Make sure Chrome is signed in to your tester Gmail
04
Tap "Become a tester" on the Google Play page

You will see a button that says "Become a tester". Tap it and wait a few seconds. The page will refresh and show an Install button. You only need to do this once.

05
Tap "Install" to download the app

After becoming a tester, tap "Install". The app will download and install normally on your phone, just like any other app from the Play Store.

⏱ Installation takes about 30–60 seconds
06
Explore and send your feedback

Try every button, every feature, every screen. If something crashes, freezes, looks wrong, or feels confusing, take a screenshot and send it to aronmickoboquiren@gmail.com.

💬 Honest feedback = better app for everyone

Common Questions

Frequently asked questions.

Everything you need to know about beta testing these apps.

Why can't I see the "Install" button on Google Play?
You need to tap "Become a tester" first before the Install button appears. Make sure you are opening the link in Google Chrome on your Android phone, and that Chrome is signed in to the exact Gmail you registered as a tester.
Can I test on iPhone or on a computer?
These apps are currently Android only. They will not work on iPhone, iPad, or computer. iOS support may be added in the future.
Do I need to test all 4 apps?
No — you can test one, two, three, or all four apps. There is no pressure. Any feedback you can give, even for just one app, is incredibly valuable and appreciated.
What should I do if the app crashes or freezes?
Please take a screenshot and send it to aronmickoboquiren@gmail.com with a brief description of what you were doing before it crashed.
Can I share the testing links with my friends or family?
Please do not share these links publicly or with people outside this testing program. The testing spots are limited, and these are pre-release versions not yet ready for the general public.
How do I update the app when a new version is released?
For major updates, you may need to uninstall the old version first and then install the new version. Minor updates should appear automatically in your Play Store updates.
Are these apps safe? Do they collect my personal data?
Yes, these apps are safe. Most work completely offline and do not require an account or collect personal data. They are free of ads and subscriptions.
I want to give positive feedback too. Is that useful?
Absolutely! Knowing what feels good and what works well is just as important as knowing what is broken. Please share anything you loved. 🙏
