Why This Exists

A note from the founder.

I know what anxiety feels like. I know what it is like to wake up with your heart racing, not knowing why. I know what panic attacks feel like in the middle of the day when everything seems fine on the outside.

Step by Step Guide

How to join the test.

Follow these steps carefully. It takes less than 2 minutes and you only need to do this once per app.

01
Open Google Chrome and sign in with your Gmail

On your Android phone, open Google Chrome. Make sure you are signed in with the Gmail account you registered as a tester. The testing access is tied to your Google account.

02
Copy the testing link of the app you want

Scroll down to the apps section below. Find the app you want to test and tap the "Copy Link" button.

📋 One tap to copy — no typing needed
03
Paste the link in Google Chrome's address bar

Tap the address bar at the top, long-press and tap "Paste", then press Enter. This takes you directly to the Google Play testing page.

📱 Make sure Chrome is signed in to your tester Gmail
04
Tap "Become a tester" on the Google Play page

You will see a button that says "Become a tester". Tap it and wait a few seconds. You only need to do this once.

05
Tap "Install" to download the app

After becoming a tester, tap "Install". The app will download and install normally.

⏱ Installation takes about 30–60 seconds
06
Explore and send your feedback

Try every button, every feature, every screen. If something crashes or feels confusing, take a screenshot and send it to aronmickoboquiren@gmail.com.

💬 Honest feedback = better app for everyone

Before You Begin

Important things to know.

🔒
Keep Links Private

These links are for selected testers only. Please do not share them publicly or with anyone not part of this program.

📱
Android Phones Only

These apps are currently on Android only. Open the links on your Android phone via Google Chrome for installation to work correctly.

🧪
Beta Versions

These are testing versions. Some features may still be in development. Bugs are expected. That is exactly why you are here.

💬
Report Everything

Crashes, bugs, confusing buttons, slow loading. All feedback matters. No report is too small. Screenshots are very helpful.

⚕️
Wellness Companions

These apps are wellness companions, not medical treatments. If you are in crisis, please call 988 or contact a licensed professional.
